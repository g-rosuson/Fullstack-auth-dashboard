# Tabs

Tabbed surfaces expose named tabs and show one tab’s content at a time.

- **FR-UI-TAB-001** — A tabbed surface shall expose each tab with an accessible name.
- **FR-UI-TAB-002** — Selecting a tab shall show that tab’s content and shall hide the other tabs’ content.
- **FR-UI-TAB-003** — Until the user selects another tab, a tabbed surface shall show the first tab’s content.
